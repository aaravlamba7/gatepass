COLLEGE ENTRY & EXIT

A simple digital gate pass

Students show their pass at the gate. The guard checks the college ID and records an entry or exit.

Demo version: The sample students below are fictional. Scan records are saved only in this browser.

How to use it

  1. Show the QR pass to the guard.
  2. The guard checks the student's physical college ID.
  3. The guard scans the QR and selects entry or exit.
